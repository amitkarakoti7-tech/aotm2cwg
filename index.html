Jaismine Lamboria

Boxing · Women's 57kg

Added Commonwealth gold to her World Championship title, and carried India's flag at the closing ceremony.

Delivered India's first-ever Commonwealth Games judo gold medals — both on the same historic day.

Neeraj Chopra

Athletics · Javelin Throw

Marked his Commonwealth return after missing Birmingham 2022 through injury, claiming silver in the men's javelin.

Gulveer Singh

Athletics · 10,000m & 5,000m

India's only double medallist in Glasgow, winning silver in the 10,000m and bronze in the 5,000m.

Tejaswin Shankar

Athletics · Decathlon

Won India's first-ever Commonwealth Games decathlon medal after two demanding days of competition.

Dilip Gavit is one of India's most successful Para Athletes. His journey from a small village in Maharashtra to winning a Gold Medal at the Commonwealth Games 2026 is truly inspiring.
